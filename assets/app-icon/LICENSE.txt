Pulso app icon (effectcraft.svg, effectcraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in this
directory, and the web favicon where the project has one).

The icon is a portrait of Eadweard Muybridge cut out, converted to black and white and placed over the
app colour. It belongs to the Nodalia icon family: one historical figure per app.

Source portrait: "Portrait of Eadweard Muybridge, pioneer of early action photography.png", from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portrait_of_Eadweard_Muybridge,_pioneer_of_early_action_photography.png).
Status of the source: public domain.
